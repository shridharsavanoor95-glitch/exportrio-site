Global Export Markets

Exportrio exports Indian non-basmati rice to high-volume international markets, with a strategic focus on West Africa. Our operations are led by strong and consistent demand from Benin, supported by selective supply to other African and international markets based on buyer requirements and logistics feasibility.

Primary High-Volume Markets

🇧🇯 Benin (Primary Focus)

Benin is Exportrio’s primary export destination for Indian non-basmati rice and a key gateway to West Africa. The country represents one of the largest import markets for Indian rice, with strong demand for IR64, parboiled rice, and long-grain white rice. Shipments are executed on FOB India terms and support both domestic consumption and regional redistribution.

🇸🇳 Senegal

A major West African rice-consuming market with stable demand for parboiled and non-basmati rice for mass consumption.

🇬🇳 Guinea

A growing African import market with increasing preference for competitively priced Indian non-basmati rice.

🇹🇬 Togo

A strategic West African port market supporting inland distribution and regional trade flows.

🇰🇪 Kenya

A significant East African importer of IR64 and long-grain rice for retail and institutional buyers.
